### 👨‍💻 About Me

I am a developer and maker based in **Kerala, India**, building across the entire stack — from low-level microcontrollers and custom hardware drivers to game engines, creative AI tools, and resilient web platforms.

I love working with **constrained systems** — whether that means squeezing real-time physics and kinematics into an ESP32 microcontroller, creating lightweight local AI workflows for low-spec machines, or designing tactile, responsive web experiences.

- 🤖 **Hardware & Embedded:** Building bipedal walkers, Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) controllers, and custom I2C display drivers.
- 🎮 **Game Development & 3D:** Building RPG gameplay systems in Unity (C#) and modeling/rigging assets in Blender.
- 🧠 **Applied & Creative AI:** Architecting multi-agent systems, edge intelligence platforms, and local generative tooling.
- 🌐 **Full-Stack Engineering:** Developing high-performance, accessible applications with modern web and API frameworks.
